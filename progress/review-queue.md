# Spaced Review Queue

When a topic first reaches 🟨/✅, add a row with reviews due at +2, +7, +21, +60 days.
`/today` runs due items (≤10 min) before new material. On each review mark ✅ (recalled well) or ❌.
A ❌ resets that topic to +2 days and adds a weak-area entry.
