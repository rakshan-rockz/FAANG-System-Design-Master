# Intake interview

Goal: fill `progress/profile.md` so depth, examples and interviewer style fit this learner.
~30–45 min. Content is never reduced based on intake; only emphasis, examples and difficulty change.

1. Explain in 3 lines why this matters (level and target companies change depth expectations and
   interviewer style; background lets examples connect to systems they know).
2. Ask the profile sections **a few questions at a time**: targets → availability/rhythm →
   background → self-assessment → preferences. Probe vague answers ("You said 'some Kafka':
   did you operate it or just produce to it?").
3. Ask 4–5 short calibration questions (unscored), e.g. "10M requests/day is how many per second?",
   "What happens when a DB replica lags?", "What's a cache stampede?", "L4 vs L7 load balancer?".
   Note answers in the Mentor column.
4. State the calibration: depth bar for their target level (L4: correct & complete; L5: drives the
   interview, deep-dives 2 components, quantifies trade-offs; L6: frames ambiguity, cost, evolution,
   operations), company-format emphasis (PLAN §7), and where you'll push hardest.
5. Write `progress/profile.md` and update `progress/STATUS.md` (Started date; next = 0.0.2 baseline).
